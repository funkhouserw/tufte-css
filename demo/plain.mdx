# A plain post

This post uses only Markdown, as most posts will. No components or imports are needed, and the file still comes out as an article with sections, figures and margins in the right places. Write *emphasis*, **strong**, `inline code` and [links](https://github.com/edwardtufte/tufte-css) the usual way.

A second paragraph makes sure the measure and the rhythm between paragraphs look right. Lines should sit comfortably between forty-five and eighty characters at every width, with the same cream paper and off-black text as any other post.

## A section heading

Each second-level heading starts a new section. Third-level headings stay within the section, and anything deeper is a sign the post needs another look.

### A lower-level heading

Lists work as they do anywhere else:

- The first item on a short list.
- The second item, long enough to wrap onto another line at most widths so the hanging indent can be checked.
- The third item.

1. A numbered list.
2. With a second entry.

> A block quote is lightly styled and set apart from the surrounding text, and it can run to a few lines without any special treatment.
